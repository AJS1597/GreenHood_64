GreenHood 64 is a dungeon crawling first person stealth game.  You explore randomly generated castles to find kings to kill and steal from.  These castles have guards, who will try to stop you.  You can take out these enemies loud or quiet.  You can then use the gold you gain to buy upgrades.  The small loop is exploring the castle, finding loot/crowns, and exfiltrating with loot.  The big loop is buying upgrades, spending crowns, and getting stronger.  The first ten minutes of planned gameplay is enter the castle -> find  a bit of gold -> die. This will probably take ~4 minutes. This will stress the importance of exfiltrating if you are weak.  Then, they will go back in, find more gold, and leave. This will probably take another 4 minutes.  Then, the player will end the 10 minutes by using this gold to upgrade the bow to a slightly better one.

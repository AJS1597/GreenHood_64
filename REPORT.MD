Our first playtest session took place directly after class.  This session focused on game/player feel and map generation, fine tuning the player movement and also method in which the castle dungeons are built. This included fixing orientation of generated walls, stairs, and chests.  Also, a lot of Z-Fighting as occurring at the start which was so apparent it made the game experience worse.  This also involved messing with ways of making the player feel faster without changing how fast the player actually is for balance reasons.

The second most notable session took place on Thursday, involving major work on combat. We messed around with the boss fight and guard/enemy AI and alert state.  We found it was too easy, so we made guards a little harder.  We also found that the map was too large and lead to doubling back as it all felt very same-y, so we added a mini-map with fog of war.  This allows you to realize you are doubling back without giving away complete information of the dungeon to the player.
